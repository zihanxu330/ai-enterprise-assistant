# Northwind Solutions Employee Handbook

## Welcome

Northwind Solutions is a fictional logistics technology company providing order dispatch and fleet management software to enterprise customers across Asia. This handbook summarizes policies for all employees.

## Work Hours

Standard working hours are Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM (local time), with a one-hour lunch break. Remote work is allowed on Fridays after completing a probation period of three months.

## Leave Policy

Employees accrue 12 days of paid annual leave per year, plus 5 days of sick leave. Annual leave can be carried over to the next year up to 5 days. National holidays follow the local calendar.

## Benefits

All employees receive medical insurance, a retirement savings plan, and an annual learning stipend of 2000 CNY for professional courses and certifications. Engineers also get an annual hardware allowance of 3000 CNY.

## Code of Conduct

Employees must follow the company's code of conduct, which prohibits sharing confidential customer data with third parties without authorization. All customer data must be stored in the approved cloud region.
